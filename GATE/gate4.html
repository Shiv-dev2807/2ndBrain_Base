01 Programming-First Syllabus

The subject order is completely rigged. You will master Programming & DS, Algorithms, DBMS, OS, and Networks *first*. By September, you will have finished 80% of what high-paying product companies test, while simultaneously knocking out the highest-weight GATE subjects.

02 The Add-on Protocol

Once you finish a GATE subject block, apply the "Add-on". Finished GATE Algorithms? The add-on is LeetCode Hard/Codeforces DP, BFS, and B-Trees. Finished GATE DBMS? The add-on is designing an IPL Data Analytics database schema.

03 AIR 1 Precision

Once the programming foundation is locked, we pivot hard to pure theoretical/hardware GATE subjects (Discrete Math, TOC, Compilers, Digital Logic). AIR 1 leaves zero gaps. You master the hardware logic that drives IoT microcontrollers (like Arduino) just as deeply as you master Python.

04 Protect the machine

7 hours of sleep isn't rest, it's a performance setting. The cognitive load of switching between advanced dynamic programming and GATE theory requires deep consolidation. Sleep is non-negotiable.

The Struggle Protocol: Every CP problem (N-Queens, Graph theory, etc.) gets a 40-minute struggle window. Do not look at the editorial. The struggle wires the neural pathways required to crack both Trilogy SDE assessments and 2-mark GATE NATs.
